Put Vito Taxi's own photographs here:

    vito-1.jpg   the Vito, outside, clean — ideally at golden hour or at night with the lights on
    vito-2.jpg   the cabin / back seats, empty and spotless
    vito-3.jpg   the driver(s), next to the car, looking at the camera

Landscape, about 4:3, 1600px wide is plenty. Compress before adding (squoosh.app, ~200–300 KB each).

The gallery under "The Vito" stays hidden until at least one of these files loads,
so an empty folder is a valid state. Rename or add more in `photos=` inside CFG
at the top of tools/build.py, then run `python3 tools/build.py`.
